Business Setup Resources

Checklists and official starting points for U.S. founders preparing to file or compare services.

Business setup checklist

  • Before filing: choose a state, check the proposed business name, and confirm agent arrangements.
  • After filing: keep the accepted document, check EIN and licensing needs, and record annual deadlines.
  • Before paying a provider: confirm what’s included, how long a quote applies, and what renews.

For founders outside the U.S.

Ask each provider whether it accepts applicants in your country of residence and what verification it requires. Check the IRS EIN page for application options. Verify bank or payment-provider eligibility separately.
