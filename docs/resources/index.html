Business Setup Resources

Checklists and official starting points for U.S. founders preparing to file or compare services.

Business setup checklist

  • Before filing: choose a state, check the proposed business name, and confirm agent arrangements.
  • After filing: keep the accepted document, check EIN and licensing needs, and record annual deadlines.
  • Before paying a provider: confirm what’s included, how long a quote applies, and what renews.

For founders outside the U.S.

Ask each provider whether it accepts applicants in your country of residence and what verification it requires. Check the IRS EIN page for application options. Verify bank or payment-provider eligibility separately.

Official starting points

  • SBA: Register your business
  • IRS: Employer Identification Numbers
  • State filing guides

Questions for a provider inquiry

Use this starting point: “I’m considering a [state] LLC. Please confirm applicant eligibility, the total first-year cost with state fees, the registered agent renewal price, any optional charges, and what documents I will receive.”

Records to keep

  • Keep the state’s filing receipt and accepted formation document together.
  • Save the registered agent agreement and its renewal terms.
  • If applicable, keep the IRS EIN confirmation with your company records.
  • Record annual state obligations, optional subscription renewals, and local permit dates.

Compare sources

Use state and federal agencies for filing rules and deadlines. Use the provider’s written quote and terms for package contents, pricing, renewal, cancellation and refund details.
