FOUNDER RESOURCES

Before you apply.

Review a checklist and find the right official source for your question.

Browse resources →
What should I prepare before filing an LLC?

Start with a proposed business name, a formation state, and your registered agent details. Check the state’s official filing instructions before submitting.

What should I compare when choosing an agent?

Review state coverage, the renewal price, how official documents are delivered, and which add-ons cost extra.

Where can I read the official EIN instructions?

Visit the IRS’s EIN guidance and check the application route for your situation.

Can I form in one state and operate in another?

You may need to register in an additional state where you conduct business. See the SBA’s registration guide and check the requirements with both state offices.

What should a provider quote include?

Ask for the state filing fee, preparation charge, annual agent renewal, mail or address add-ons and the cancellation terms.
