Registered Agent Services

Understand what a registered agent handles and what to ask a provider before choosing one.

What the agent does

A registered agent receives official papers for the business and must be located in the state of registration. Confirm which documents the provider scans, forwards, or stores. See the SBA registration guide.

What to compare

  • Ask for the first-year price and the rate when the service renews.
  • Ask how quickly notices appear and whether forwarding costs extra.
  • Confirm whether the price includes only agent service or a separate business mailing service.
  • Ask how to cancel or change agents and which filing is required.

If you are switching

Review your state's instructions, coordinate the effective date with the new agent, and keep the state's confirmation. Browse State Guides

Registered agent vs. business mailing service

A registered agent is designated to receive official documents. If you also need mail scanning or a business address, ask whether these are separate services and confirm the kinds of mail the provider accepts.

Questions to send a provider

  • What does the service cost in the first year and at renewal?
  • How will I be alerted about an official document, and when will it be available?
  • Are physical forwarding, extra recipients or a change-of-agent filing included?
  • How do I cancel the subscription after the state accepts a replacement agent?

Keep the appointment and renewal records

Keep the appointment confirmation with the business records. Review the delivery email, account access and renewal date before assuming notices will reach the right person.
