EIN Assistance

Gather the details for your federal Employer Identification Number application and review your options.

Before applying

Confirm the legal name and formation details on your documents. Determine who the responsible party is and check which details the IRS requires for your application.

Apply through the IRS

The IRS issues EINs directly at no charge. The application method can depend on where your principal business is located and the responsible party’s information. Check the official IRS EIN instructions before choosing a method.

If you use a paid service

Ask which support the fee buys, who will prepare and submit the application, and how you will receive the confirmation. Read the IRS Form SS-4 instructions before authorizing anyone to act on your behalf.

Online and international applications

Check the IRS online tool's eligibility requirements first. It asks for the responsible party’s SSN or ITIN and is for eligible applicants with a principal place of business in the United States or a U.S. territory. If the principal place of business is outside the United States, follow the international application instructions on the official IRS EIN page.

Prepare before submitting

  • Use the name the state accepted rather than a trade name in the legal-name field.
  • Check that the address, entity type and reason for applying match your records.
  • Use the current Form SS-4 instructions if using a route that calls for the form.
  • Keep the IRS confirmation and avoid submitting another application while you are following up on the first.

Cost and scope of paid help

Ask what the assistance fee covers, whether you need to sign an authorization, and how the service will deliver the IRS confirmation. The IRS itself issues EINs without an application fee.
