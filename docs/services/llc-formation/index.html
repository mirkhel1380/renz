LLC Formation

Prepare your information, understand the filing process, and see what to check before ordering a formation package.

Before you file

  • Identify the state where you plan to operate and check whether registration there or in another state may be necessary.
  • Check the state naming rules and verify availability with the filing office.
  • Confirm your registered agent and the addresses the filing form requires.

Ask for an itemized quote

Separate state filing fees from the service charge. Ask which documents the provider prepares, whether the registered agent is included, and how long any introductory pricing lasts.

After filing

Keep the filed formation document and confirmation. Review your operating agreement, EIN needs, licensing, and annual obligations. Start with the SBA guide to registering your business.

Related services

Review Registered Agent Services and EIN Assistance.

What an itemized estimate should show

  • Government filing fee, with any expedite charge shown separately.
  • Provider preparation fee and which documents are included.
  • Registered agent, address or mail services, and how each subscription renews.
  • How the provider handles corrections, cancellations and refunds.

Business addresses and documents

Check how the form distinguishes the principal office, mailing address and registered office. Ask the provider whether any address included in the package can be used for the specific purpose you need.

Your formation timeline

Ask the provider to separate its preparation time from the state's processing time. After acceptance, download the filed document and verify the legal name and filing date before using either on other applications.
