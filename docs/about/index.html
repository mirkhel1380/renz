About Renz

Renz gives founders practical starting points for U.S. company formation, registered agent services and EIN preparation.

What Renz covers

Renz focuses on U.S. business setup. The site brings together service explanations, questions to ask providers, state filing links, and checklists for common tasks. It is written for people planning a new company or handling a next step for an existing one.

Who the guides are for

  • First-time founders who want to understand how a formation quote is put together.
  • Owners comparing a registered agent’s renewal price and document delivery.
  • Founders outside the United States gathering questions about eligibility, documents and EIN application options.

How we organize the information

State and federal agency pages support the sections about filing and application rules. Provider websites are linked alongside the directory cards so you can ask the provider to confirm pricing, eligibility, and what a package includes.

How to use the provider directory

Compare upfront charges, renewal costs, state coverage, eligibility and what you will receive. The order in the directory is a listing order. Ask each provider the same questions and keep its latest written quote with your records.

What the checklist does

Pick a state and service and Renz will generate a short planning checklist in your browser. You can then open the relevant guide, consult the official filing office or contact a provider directly.

Keep reading

Start with the Renz Blog for practical examples and the state directory for filing links. The Contact page explains how to prepare a setup question and where to direct it.
