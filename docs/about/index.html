About Renz

Renz helps founders organize their next steps for setting up a business in the United States.

What you can explore

The site covers LLC formation, registered agents, and EIN applications. The service pages describe what to prepare and which questions to ask.

How to use this information

Use the checklists as starting points. Check official forms and provider terms before filing or paying. For legal or tax advice about your specific business, speak with a qualified professional.

Who the guides are for

Renz is for people preparing to start a U.S. business or reviewing common next steps for an existing company. It covers formation, registered agent services and EIN preparation.

Where the information comes from

State and federal filing instructions are linked in the relevant guides. The provider directory links to company sites so visitors can confirm their latest package details directly.

How to compare providers on Renz

Use the same criteria for each package: upfront costs, ongoing costs, state coverage, eligibility and what the provider delivers.
