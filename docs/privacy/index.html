Privacy

How Renz handles checklist choices, routine website requests, and visits to linked providers or government agencies.

Scope

This notice covers the Renz website, including the blog, service and state guides, provider directory, and setup checklist. A site reached through an external link has its own privacy practices.

Information used by the checklist

The checklist uses the formation state and the service you choose to prepare a list in your current browser. The checklist does not submit those selections as a message or keep its own saved copy. Its fields do not request names, email addresses, tax numbers, documents, or payment details.

State selections in page links

The state directory can include a state name in the URL so the checklist opens with that state selected. The requested URL can appear in browser history and ordinary hosting logs. The link does not carry a name or a personally identifying application number.

Hosting and access

When a page is loaded, the hosting service may process routine request information, such as the requested URL, browser information and IP address, to deliver and protect the site. If a private preview asks you to sign in, the host manages that sign-in under its own terms. Renz’s checklist does not receive your password.

Cookies and browser storage

The public-facing Renz pages and checklist do not set their own advertising cookies or save a checklist in browser storage. A host may use its own access or security technology, such as when signing in to an owner-only preview.

Links to other websites

The site includes links to formation providers and government agencies. Once you visit another site, check its privacy information before entering personal, company or payment details. Renz does not receive an application you submit on those sites through the provider directory.

Messages and sensitive documents

The current Contact page is informational and does not accept messages or file uploads. Do not put tax IDs, passports, payment information or official documents into links or use them as search terms on Renz.

Using the site and reading this notice

You can use the formation guides and blog without submitting a checklist. See the Contact page for other ways to find the right source for a question. Review this notice when a new feature changes how the site handles information.
