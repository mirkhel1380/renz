Privacy

How the current Renz site uses selections made in the setup tool.

Setup tool

Your service selection and the resulting checklist are processed in the current browser. The checklist does not send a message or save the selection in its own storage.

External websites

Official agencies and providers linked from Renz operate under their own privacy terms. Review the destination before entering personal, payment, or company information.

Information the checklist uses

The checklist takes a formation state and service choice. It does not ask for a name, email address, tax ID, passport number, payment details, or documents.

State directory links

Choosing a state from the state directory can put the state name in the page URL to prefill the checklist. That URL can appear in browser history and in ordinary hosting logs.

Third-party visits and future forms

If you follow a link to a filing office or provider, their terms and privacy information apply on that site. Direct contact is not enabled in this version. Review this page if a contact feature is added.
