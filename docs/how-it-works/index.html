How Renz Works

Go from a general idea to a checklist you can use when researching or choosing a service.

1. Choose a state

Select a formation state or leave it undecided. The State Guides explain where to start.

2. Pick the task

Choose LLC formation, registered agent services, EIN assistance, or help deciding.

3. Review your checklist

The homepage form builds a brief checklist in your browser. Use it to gather information and prepare questions for a provider.

4. Confirm details with the provider

Before paying, confirm eligibility, total initial price, renewal terms, and what the company will deliver. See provider profiles.
