How Renz Works

Go from a general idea to a checklist you can use when researching or choosing a service.

1. Choose a state

Select a formation state or leave it undecided. The State Guides explain where to start.

2. Pick the task

Choose LLC formation, registered agent services, EIN assistance, or help deciding.

3. Review your checklist

The homepage form builds a brief checklist in your browser. Use it to gather information and prepare questions for a provider.

4. Confirm details with the provider

Before paying, confirm eligibility, total initial price, renewal terms, and what the company will deliver. See provider profiles.

Example: starting a Wyoming LLC

Select Wyoming and LLC Formation. Use the checklist to verify the name, confirm an agent, and review the filing instructions. Then open the Wyoming guide for state sources and the annual report timing.

Example: changing an agent

Select the state where your LLC is already registered and choose Registered Agent. Ask the replacement about effective dates and document handling, and check the state’s change-of-agent requirements before cancelling the existing service.

What you can do after the checklist

Open the service guide for details, read the relevant official filing page, or contact a provider for a written quote. The checklist is a planning tool and the site does not submit your selections as a request.
