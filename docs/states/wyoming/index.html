Wyoming LLC Setup

Find the Wyoming filing starting point and see what to review before and after forming.

Prepare the filing

Use the Wyoming Secretary of State’s business forms page to find the LLC Articles of Organization and related instructions. Review registered agent and address requirements before filing.

Budget for the next year

Look up the state's annual report requirements and current fees. If you hire a registered agent, ask for their renewal cost separately from the state's charges.

Before choosing Wyoming

If you will operate elsewhere, use the SBA registration guide to check whether another registration may be needed. Compare other states

Annual report timing

Wyoming generally makes the annual report due on the first day of the anniversary month. For example, an LLC formed in May would use May 1 as its recurring report date. Confirm the due date for your business through the Wyoming Secretary of State’s business FAQ.

Check the official documents

See the state's LLC Articles of Organization, including registered agent information. Use the Wyoming business portal to look up the filed entity and current filing options.

Wyoming setup checklist

  • Check the proposed name and review the state’s address fields.
  • Confirm your agent appointment and the provider’s renewal price.
  • Keep the Articles and the state's filing confirmation.
  • Record the annual report due date and any separate provider renewal date.
