Delaware LLC Setup

Start with Delaware’s official formation instructions and review costs beyond the initial filing.

Prepare the filing

Delaware's formation guide walks through choosing an entity and a Delaware registered agent. Review the Certificate of Formation and the filing options it links to.

Review ongoing obligations

Check Delaware’s current LLC tax guidance before paying a provider. Ask for the first-year and renewal costs so the initial filing price does not obscure ongoing expenses.

Compare with your operating state

Check whether activities in another state could trigger a second registration. Compare other states

Plan for the annual LLC tax

Delaware’s Division of Corporations says LLCs pay an annual tax by June 1 and do not file an annual report for this tax. Review the Delaware Division of Corporations FAQ for the amount and rules before setting your calendar.

Prepare before requesting a quote

  • Check the LLC name and confirm your Delaware registered agent.
  • Ask if the service supplies an accepted copy of the Certificate of Formation.
  • List the annual state tax and the agent renewal separately.

Keep the file number

Once the entity is registered, keep the file number with the formation documents. Use the Delaware entity status options when you need to check a record or find out how to request official proof of status.
