Texas LLC Setup

Review the Texas filing forms, registered agent details, and follow-up requirements before ordering a service.

Prepare the filing

Find the Texas LLC Certificate of Formation on the Secretary of State forms page. Review the instructions for the registered agent, registered office, and business information.

Check post-formation requirements

Review Texas Comptroller franchise tax guidance and relevant local permits for your activities. Put applicable filing deadlines on your calendar.

Ask for a complete quote

Compare the state filing fee, preparation cost, and registered agent renewal before selecting a package. Browse provider profiles

Use the current Form 205 instructions

The Texas Secretary of State’s Form 205 instructions walk through the entity name, registered agent and office, governing authority, and organizer details. Check those fields before completing a provider's intake.

After registration

Keep the accepted Certificate of Formation and verify your address and governing details. Check the Texas Comptroller’s franchise tax page for rules that apply to your reporting year and business.

Texas setup checklist

  • Identify who will manage the LLC before preparing Form 205.
  • Confirm registered agent consent and the registered office details.
  • Check any local permits or tax registrations relevant to your activities.
  • Record the state obligations and provider renewal dates separately.
