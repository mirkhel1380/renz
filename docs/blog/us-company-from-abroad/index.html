International Founders

Starting a U.S. Company from Abroad

A pre-order checklist for non-U.S. residents comparing formation providers and related services.

Check service eligibility first

Give the provider your country of residence and ask whether it accepts applicants there. Request a written list of acceptable identification and address documents. Check what happens to your payment if verification is unsuccessful.

Keep addresses and roles clear

Ask which company addresses each filing or service needs and whether a registered agent package includes a separate mailing service. Confirm what kinds of mail can be accepted, scanned or forwarded.

Plan EIN, banking and payments separately

Read the IRS application options before ordering EIN assistance. Separately ask the bank or payment company you plan to use about its country, document and identity requirements before relying on that account for your business.

Ask about reporting before you form

A tax adviser familiar with U.S. cross-border businesses can help you determine which federal, state and home-country obligations apply. Share the proposed ownership, location and business activity when getting advice.

Checklist before paying

  • Ask the formation provider for a dated quote and confirm residence eligibility.
  • Check the official filing process and expected records.
  • Keep the formation, EIN and account-opening steps on separate checklists.
