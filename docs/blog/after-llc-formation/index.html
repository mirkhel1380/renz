After Formation

What to Do After Your LLC Is Formed

A starting checklist for company records, renewals, and official filings after the state accepts your LLC.

Make a company records folder

Save the accepted state filing, receipt, agent details, operating agreement if applicable, and EIN confirmation if issued. Verify the legal name and spelling across the documents before using them for another application.

Build a calendar before renewals arrive

  • Find and note the state reporting or tax deadlines that apply to your entity.
  • Record the registered agent renewal date and any address-service subscriptions.
  • Check whether your business activity requires city, county or state licenses and when they renew.

Verify deadlines in the right place

Rules differ by state and entity type. Start with the Wyoming Secretary of State FAQ, the Delaware Division of Corporations FAQ or the Texas Comptroller franchise tax page as relevant to your company.

If you operate in another state

Check whether the state where you operate requires a separate registration. The SBA registration guide outlines how to look up foreign qualification. Browse the Renz state guides.
