EIN

EIN Options for U.S. and International Applicants

Find the official IRS application route and gather the information needed before you apply.

Start with the IRS eligibility guidance

The IRS’s online application is for eligible applicants whose principal business is in the United States or a U.S. territory. The IRS also publishes a separate path for applicants whose principal place of business is outside the United States. Check the current instructions before you begin.

Prepare your information

  • Use the name shown on the accepted formation document.
  • Confirm your entity type, mailing address, and reason for applying.
  • Identify the responsible party and use the current Form SS-4 instructions if your route calls for the form.
  • Keep your IRS confirmation with the state filing.

When paying for help

There is no IRS charge to issue an EIN. If a provider charges to help with your application, ask what it will prepare, whether you will need to sign an authorization, and how it will send you the confirmation.

Common problems before you apply

  • Check your approved legal name against your formation document instead of copying the name from a draft order.
  • Check which application route the IRS directs you to based on the principal place of business and the responsible party’s information.
  • If an application is pending, use the IRS follow-up instructions before submitting again.

Save and use the confirmation

Keep the IRS confirmation with the company’s formation records and limit access to the people who need it. If it is lost, consult the IRS EIN confirmation guidance for current options.
