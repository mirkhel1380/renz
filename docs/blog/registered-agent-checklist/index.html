Registered Agent

What to Ask Before Choosing a Registered Agent

A practical checklist for state coverage, renewal costs, and delivery of official documents.

Confirm the appointment

Confirm that the provider covers the state where your entity is registered. Ask for the exact information your state form requires and check whether the filing fee for appointing or changing the agent is included.

Test the document workflow

  • Ask how soon documents typically appear after receipt.
  • Check whether notifications can go to more than one authorized person.
  • Ask what mail the provider accepts and what it costs to forward physical documents.
  • Confirm how to access previous documents if you move to another provider.

Check renewal and optional services

Ask when the agent subscription renews and what it costs. If you also need a business mailing address, ask for a separate quote and check the permitted uses of that address.

If you already have an agent

Ask the new provider how to coordinate the change and when the appointment takes effect. Check the state’s filing instructions, then keep confirmation of the change with your business records.

Ask for a real example of the process

Give the provider a scenario: “If you receive a document with a response deadline on a Friday, how will you notify me and where will I find the document?” Ask how to add a backup contact and whether there is any additional cost.

Set reminders before you renew

  • Record both the state’s filing deadlines and the date the agent subscription renews.
  • Confirm how to change the account email if staff or ownership changes.
  • Keep a copy of the appointment confirmation and the cancellation terms outside the provider dashboard.
