After Formation

Build an Annual Compliance Calendar for Your LLC

Track state requirements, registered agent renewal, and related business deadlines in one place.

Start with the state’s rules

Check the state where you formed and any additional states where the LLC is registered. Identify which obligations are tied to a calendar date, which depend on the formation date, and which depend on your business circumstances.

See why deadlines differ

Wyoming generally ties its annual report to the first day of the anniversary month. Delaware lists June 1 as the deadline for its annual LLC tax and says LLCs do not file an annual report for that tax. Texas publishes franchise tax reporting guidance separately. Check the official links before adding deadlines to your calendar.

Record state charges and subscriptions separately

  • Keep one calendar entry per filing or renewal.
  • Record the agency or provider, what is due, and who is responsible.
  • Add a reminder ahead of each deadline and retain the receipt or confirmation.
  • Review the entries if you change agents, move or register in a second state.

Official state resources

Use the Wyoming Secretary of State FAQ, Delaware Division of Corporations FAQ and Texas Comptroller franchise tax guidance as applicable.
