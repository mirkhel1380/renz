Business Structure

LLC or Corporation? Questions to Ask Before Filing

Compare how you plan to run the business, bring in owners, raise money, and manage recurring obligations.

Ownership and management

An LLC generally refers to its owners as members. A corporation issues shares to shareholders. Decide who will own the company, who will make day-to-day decisions, and how you will document changes in control.

Plans for investment

If you expect to raise outside capital, ask prospective investors and your adviser whether they have requirements for the structure. Compare those needs with the ownership arrangements you want today.

Separate state structure from federal tax treatment

Formation is a state-level process. Federal tax treatment depends on the entity and its elections. Check IRS guidance for the facts of your company before choosing a structure solely because you heard it is taxed a particular way.

Before asking a provider to file

  • List current and future owners and how you expect to split responsibilities.
  • Ask about what governance documents are included and which recurring filings apply.
  • Confirm the entity type on your order before paying.
