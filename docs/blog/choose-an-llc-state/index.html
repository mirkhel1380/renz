State Selection

How to Choose a State for Your LLC

Compare where you will operate, what state filings apply, and the total cost of maintaining your company.

Start where you will do business

List where the LLC will have an office, employees or regular operations. Ask the relevant filing offices how they apply their registration rules to your facts. Forming in one state may lead to a second registration in a state where the business operates.

Build a two-year comparison

  • List the filing fee and any optional expedited processing.
  • Add the registered agent price for each state where the business needs an agent.
  • Check likely annual reports, taxes or related charges with each state office.
  • Record any registration, record request or certificate fee associated with operating in another state.

Apply it to a simple scenario

If you plan to run the business from Texas but are considering forming in Wyoming, check what it would take to operate in Texas before comparing quotes. Include both states’ requirements in your estimate if the business needs both registrations.

Use official sources

Read the SBA business registration guide and compare the Wyoming, Delaware and Texas starting guides.
