Before You Apply

What should I have ready for LLC formation?

Start with a proposed business name, a formation state, and your registered agent details. Check the state’s official filing instructions before submitting.

What should I compare when choosing an agent?

Ask about state coverage, the renewal price, how official documents are delivered, and which add-ons cost extra.

Where can I read the official EIN instructions?

Use the IRS guide to Employer Identification Numbers to check the application options for your situation.
